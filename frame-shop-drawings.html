FRAME SHOP DRAWINGS — ALL SIZES

Box-style profile: 3/4" face to the front, 1-3/4" deep sides · panel captured in through-dado · 45° miters · sizes 7×7, 8×10, 16×20, 24×30 · planned at 1,500 frames per size

1 — ONE PROFILE FITS ALL (end view of strip)

Rip: three 1-3/4" strips per 1×6 (two 1/8" kerfs — no width waste). Dado: 3/8" deep, centered in the 1-3/4" inside face, width matched to the backing. One rip setting and one dado setup serve every frame size.

Every rail, any panel size, follows one rule:

LONG POINT  = panel dimension + 3/4"

SHORT POINT = panel dimension − 3/4"

(panel + two 3/4" faces − two 3/8" dado seats). Because the dado is centered, the strip is symmetric front-to-back, so flip-nested chain cutting works for every size.

2 — RAIL CUTTING LIST (drawings share one scale)

7×7 rail — cut 4 per frame, all identical: 6,000 pcs · LP 7-3/4" / SP 6-1/4"
8×10 short rail — 2 per frame: 3,000 pcs · LP 8-3/4" / SP 7-1/4"
8×10 long rail — 2 per frame: 3,000 pcs · LP 10-3/4" / SP 9-1/4"
16×20 short rail — 2 per frame: 3,000 pcs · LP 16-3/4" / SP 15-1/4"
16×20 long rail — 2 per frame: 3,000 pcs · LP 20-3/4" / SP 19-1/4"
24×30 short rail — 2 per frame: 3,000 pcs · LP 24-3/4" / SP 23-1/4"
24×30 long rail — 2 per frame: 3,000 pcs · LP 30-3/4" / SP 29-1/4"

All miters 45°, both ends, cut across the 3/4" face. Dado runs the full length and exits at the miter faces, where it lines up with the mating rail and disappears.

4 — STRIP PATTERNS (nested chain cuts — flip the strip front-for-back after every cut)

7×7 — 8-ft strip: 13 rails (~2" scrap; one stop block for the entire size)

8×10 — 8-ft strips (long rails: 9/strip, ~3.8" scrap · short rails: 11/strip, ~5.5" scrap)

16×20 — 8-ft strips, two alternating patterns (keeps shorts and longs 1:1)

Pattern A: S-L-S-L-S (3 shorts, 2 longs, ~6.5" scrap) · Pattern B: L-S-L-S-L (2 shorts, 3 longs, ~2.5" scrap)

24×30 — 12-ft strips, two alternating patterns

Pattern A: L-S-L-S-L (3 longs, 2 shorts, ~4.5" scrap) · Pattern B: S-L-S-L-S (3 shorts, 2 longs, ~10.5" scrap)

If your yard carries 14-footers: 3 shorts + 3 longs fit one strip with ~4.4" scrap — one pattern, 1:1 built in.
